# Crit 7 reflection

**What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?**

Fixing the contracts before parallelising. With the schema, engine types and file ownership written down, workstreams did not collide, and my real MyTT data exposed what the contracts missed, so I amended them. The second breakthrough was verifying outputs myself: running things found that `pnpm test` skipped about 150 unit tests and that nothing seeded the app at boot. Reviewing two subagents' diffs later caught a repeated heading, "your your current time" and a duplicated week range before they shipped. I also measured my own claims: my first word counts undercounted, and only the browser's rendered text gave the real number.

**What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?**

I want to verify rather than delegate and hope. Directing agents moved my responsibility to reading diffs, running checks and using the product. I also fix things a marker or student would read literally: I replaced the template README, renamed "Better than what you have" to "Times that fit", and turned the disabled "Watch the recording" into a real Echo360 link. It is not perfect: home is about 190 visible words on its busiest day, over my 150 target, because it carries the calendar and week controls. I would rather say so plainly than present it as finished.
